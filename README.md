# Isaac Promos Partner

BUILD A COMPLETE PREMIUM MULTI-PAGE B2B WEBSITE FOR:

ISAAC PROMOS

IMPORTANT:

Do NOT build a generic promotional-products catalog.

Do NOT build a cheap local print-shop website.

Do NOT build a single-page landing page.

Build a sophisticated, conversion-focused, multi-page US-facing B2B website that positions Isaac Promos as a dependable custom merchandise and bulk-order solutions partner.

The company is new in the market, so the website must compensate for lower brand recognition by looking exceptionally professional, organized, trustworthy, responsive, and solution-oriented.

==================================================

1. BUSINESS POSITIONING

==================================================

Isaac Promos serves businesses, schools, organizations, teams, contractors, events, distributors, agencies, and other buyers in the United States who need custom products in bulk.

The company can help clients source, customize, produce, and fulfill products such as:

Custom T-Shirts

and other custom promotional merchandise.

Isaac Promos should NOT be positioned merely as a seller of products.

Position the brand as:

“A reliable custom merchandise partner that helps businesses figure out what to order, how to brand it, how to produce it, and how to get it done at scale.”

The website should communicate:

You do not need to already know the exact product.

You do not need to understand printing terminology.

You do not need to coordinate multiple suppliers.

You do not need to figure everything out alone.

Tell Isaac Promos what you are trying to accomplish and the team helps recommend and source the right solution.
